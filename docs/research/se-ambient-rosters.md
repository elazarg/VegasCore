# Transmission opportunities between source choices

## Service and scope

[ServiceRoster](../../Vegas/Game/ServiceRoster.lean) gives each sequential event
a fixed finite roster of player visits, protected inclusion or public sampling,
then clock ticks and expiry. Rosters may repeat owners and other players. Every
actor has a visit at its own event. The raw game retains the bounded response
interface and the specified passive observation rule at each visit.

The [full-language calendar capstone](../../Vegas/Game/SourceServiceCompilation.lean)
uses this fixed service. The [asynchronous plan](../se-schedule-generalization.md)
targets any builder satisfying timely opportunities, protected inclusion and
complete play. Its end-to-end theorem remains open. The explicit decision-packet
port is still being checked through the calendar callers.

## Decisions and retained deferrals

A resolution decides FALSE by an authenticated, evidence-free withholding
packet and TRUE by an effective opening packet. Both are actual submissions.
The [decision window](../../Vegas/Pending/ReactiveDecisionWindow.lean) represents
an opening by optional material, so FALSE needs no arbitrary payload value.
Silence is an opportunity to decide later, and expiry without an actual decision
is a public miss. The last timely owner visit requires a decision packet in the
fixed-calendar retained menu.

A source FALSE and a missed resolution can have the same application result but
different public markers and network recall. Even silence and an explicit FALSE
packet have different observable traffic. Their continuation laws cannot be
identified solely by the decoded source state. All source-representable behavior
must use one consistent packet encoding; other behavior needs an incentive or
repair argument.

[ServiceRosterTiming](../../Vegas/Game/ServiceRosterTiming.lean) supplies the
calendar timing distribution. [ServiceRosterPolicy](../../Vegas/Game/ServiceRosterPolicy.lean)
contains the timing-policy interfaces, and
[ServiceRosterDecisionSupport](../../Vegas/Game/ServiceRosterDecisionSupport.lean)
connects actual owner decisions to the roster. These generic scheduling kernels
are distinct from source SE correspondence.

## Whole traffic and later opportunities

[ReactivePolicyMixture](../../Vegas/Pending/ReactivePolicyMixture.lean) realizes
latent timing choices as actual behavioral policies. It adds no private scratch
state to the player. The optional decision-window likelihood and settlement
laws retain physical rosters, pending traffic and own recall for both Boolean
choices.

In the general theorem, a player may have deferred before the current clean
information set. A proof only about deciding at the first turn cannot establish
rationality there. Decision recall cancels that player's own past waiting
factors, but the remaining joint source and traffic reach weights still need a
stopped law. Conditioning on a rare decision site requires escape error small
relative to that site's passage probability.

Local source resolution and sample kernels retain whole native traffic,
including receipts, serial identities, observations and scheduler history.
Their equality does not automatically establish a terminal continuation law or
Bayes posterior. Timing and source state must be coupled jointly, and the
builder's public-data dependence must be respected at each actual prefix.

## Settlement and watcher evidence

[SourceServiceAudit](../../Vegas/Game/SourceServiceAudit.lean) separates sampled
signed packet evidence from public missed-decision markers. The final contract
record decides whether observed content is permitted. Lawful FALSE withholding
is retained and uncharged. A partial watcher report is not evidence that an
unreported player was silent.

Observation, report delivery and collectible collateral have separate service
obligations. Their coverage may be correlated; the watcher is not assumed to
observe every pending message. It may keep observing past player deadlines,
with punishment settled after the challenge window. Uniform conditional
collection is used only where the continuation comparison actually needs it.

One fixed deposit can deter a first departure against the entire bounded gain
from its continuation. Once that deposit is already lost, later behavior needs
a rational native continuation. Replays, off-turn communication, delayed
choices and private commitment material must each be justified by actual
observation and continuation laws; matching public outcomes is insufficient.
