# Runtime expansion proof plan

The current endpoint is native public-message execution. Further runtime work
should extend it by small, independently checked edges.

The unchanged manuscript sets the minimum theorem target. Meeting that target
requires both its strategic strength and its source-outcome observation scope;
the current completion/public-output projection alone does not imply every
utility bound over full terminal source environments. Native outcomes retain
complete policy executions. A full source decoder must be constructed from
that actual state/history, without adding a source interpreter or proof witness
to the target's execution. Eligibility and service hypotheses must also be
discharged by compilation or stated as explicit runtime capabilities.

1. Prove per-head delivery successors for every generated handler and retain
   the exact source checkpoint, completed prefix, caches, and accepted request.
   The unrestricted binding and public-choice heads are now complete under the
   actual delivery schedule: recipient delivery, reaction polls, deadline-
   aware clocking, and an unchanged relay's expiry inclusion are all covered.
   Chance and the other handler families remain to be completed.
2. Induct over whole pending-message prefixes to extract a pure source
   deviation against unchanged opponents, then linearize arbitrary
   finite-support randomized policies.
3. Compose the honest source law, deviation mixture, public-outcome guarantee,
   and same-epsilon Nash equivalence for the actual pending-message game.
4. Add adaptive public scheduling with an observation-local strategy
   translation and explicit delivery and deadline fairness.
5. State observation and deviation translations for polling, receipts, replay,
   timeout, withholding, malformed traffic, and competing valid messages.
6. Replace ideal commitments or entropy only with named concrete schemes and
   appropriate computational or distributional guarantees.
7. Introduce a ledger model for admission, ordering, clocks, receipts,
   finality, balances, and failure behavior.
8. Lower the same identified application artifact to a chosen VM and prove
   whole-handler execution, including malformed input and exceptional paths.

At each checkpoint, distinguish executable construction, local handler laws,
support refinement, progress, observation simulation, and strategic
backtranslation. A later stage should not be advertised by importing an
unconnected backend component.

## Current proof inventory

The fixed windowed block service already closes the honest law, arbitrary
randomized unilateral deviation mixture, guarantee, and same-error Nash result.
For pending delivery, the generalized service/checkpoint/prefix layer, first
poll, acceptance through delivery and reaction, and paired segment are checked.
The sample head has an exact source-chance law, complete successor checkpoints,
and a continuation law (`WindowedCheckpoint.delivery_sample_bind`). Its proof
retains initialized reachability, unchanged-player cache freshness, and the
next activation window despite arbitrary focal commands throughout its
scheduled polls, including reaction slots. The sample service's delivery
slots wait; this case does not exercise payload disclosure or prove delivery
coverage. These are the chance case's successor and continuation laws; its
two-run information argument still requires a delivery-service proof.
The checked coin-flip regression discharges the terminal continuation: the
actual completed program has its source public-result distribution under
every randomized raw replacement, with both players polled in the roster.
The unrestricted binding head now has a complete successor and continuation law
(`WindowedCheckpoint.delivery_binding_block`), and the public-choice head has
the corresponding successor theorem
(`WindowedCheckpoint.delivery_publicChoice_block`). Both include a concrete
unchanged-relay progress proof; the binding edge has regressions for both
binding-owner and recipient-side arbitrary replacements. Complete successors
and continuation laws for the other instruction families, their
information-preservation proofs, and whole-prefix extraction remain open.

The source-prefix information induction is shared across services: its local
premise compares complete supported blocks at equal successor source views.
The fixed service supplies this premise; the delivery service still requires
the corresponding per-head proofs. The generic induction is not itself a
delivery-service information theorem.

Progress and strategic simulation remain separate. A resolving reference
service can prove an honest forward law. Arbitrary scheduling requires causal
simulation from each player's declared observation. Deadline liveness further
requires that timely persistently valid messages receive a fair opportunity
before expiration; permissionless expiry alone supplies no such guarantee.
