  Completed public rows form a deterministic prefix overlay; their source label

  After forcing the focal value, the joint explicitly retains its causally

  physical marginal is insufficient.

These local arguments do not establish their global composition. In particular,
the ordinary source law must be defined for every compatible source history,
without requiring membership in a selected native history's conditional fiber.
Its static cut is determined by the public phase rank using the existing
residual-uniqueness theorems. The remaining compatibility reader must reconstruct
closed observable history and live focal/public overlays, without assuming
replay, query equality or hidden-store agreement. Its exhaustive weighted inverse
and the retained causal tail must then establish the full transport identity.

These are mathematical obligations, not checked theorem evidence.
`QualifiedBindingLaw` is still only a proposition. The clean gain comparison

## Reviewed domain and conclusion

The domain is the finite, well-formed compiled concurrent source game with
its original arbitrary finite-support joint initial law, finite binding
types, complete pending observation, reserved correlated service with one
unconditional exogenous tape prior sampled for the entire execution,
stated resource/value coverage, strict positive perturbation rates and full-support ordinary value tables. At a surviving
UNSENT binding information set I, the clean branch has no saved/current focal
binding debt and no new current-attempt exclusion. Foreign generated BAD is
conditioned out; its posterior error is restored separately. The future

raw aliases, canceled focal BAD and the original hidden tape remain present.
Tape coordinates may correlate across owners/time; no public-prefix-adaptive
block prior or tape refresh is covered. Posterior updates condition the same
original tape, rather than resample its prior.

For each legal typed current insertion v, let U be the actual native
reach-weighted terminal payoff submeasure, retaining pre-decision foreign-good
history and the original all-accepted binding gate. Let T be its total mass.
Let S be the genuine source-good reach at one full source information J, and V
that same reach followed by forced v and the actual zero-flag causal terminal
continuation. The sufficient conclusion is

    S * U(y) = T * V(y)

for every terminal payoff atom y, with one J and T across all proper typed
values and encodings. Positive T gives positive finite S; zero T yields the
all-none native law. Native and source Bayes normalization then gives exactly
the existing qualified payoff proposition. No matching cursor for arbitrary
source targets, independent initial types or desired continuation law is assumed.

## Signed comparison and assembly

lottery, and L its typed pushforward. The legal source comparison lottery is

    (1-q*p*t) D + q*p*t L.
