# Controlled delivery with a value-blind service

Analysis by Codex. This paper result combines genuine delivery risk with
finite choices to send, wait or retry. It does not assume a surely included
protected submission. It preserves exact sequential rationality and the
source law conditional on whole-game completion, while explicitly retaining
a physical-abort branch.

The important restrictions are stronger than noncollusion: service behavior
must ignore the game's data and logical choices, and every physical abort
must give each player the same sufficiently low utility. Under those
conditions a player can rationally pursue delivery without changing its
source decision analysis. The theorem is a candidate interface, not the
adopted VegasCore model or a full native raw-menu result.

## Source and proposed physical interface

**Source game.** Fix a finite perfect-recall game generated by H public serial
stages. The current stage and its logical owner, or its status as a source
chance stage, are public. H and the stage owners are fixed independently of
source actions and types. All complete source histories end after H stages.
Initial private inputs and retained bindings can be correlated. Every legal
source action remains in its original menu; terminal utilities are u_i.
Legal source chance edges have positive probability.

At an owned stage, choose the logical action once. It determines a canonical
packet. Do not choose a new value while transporting it. Source chance is
drawn on logical advancement with its original kernel, conditional on every
complete physical past. Physical records cannot predict later source draws.

**Delivery controls.** After selecting the action, one public designated
owner controls its delivery until this stage succeeds or fails. The owner
has arbitrary specified finite menus such as Send, Wait and Retry. These
operations concern only the current fixed canonical packet. A finite bound
applies to every control history. No other genuine source decision occurs
until resolution. Other physical response sites offer only silence.
Different stages can have different owners. There is no source action
redrawing, alternative private candidate selection, unrelated broadcasting,
premature opening of another commitment, fee bidding or extra packet content.

On success the original logical action takes effect with its source
transition and observations, and the next stage begins. On the first physical
failure, execution aborts and there is no further strategic choice. The
definition of failure is part of this service interface; an unsuccessful
individual send can lead to another callback if the specified control process
allows it. Final stage failure is absorbing. No protected surely successful
Open action is required.

**Every source action has the same transport process.** This includes a
source withholding or decline action, if admitted: it must use an explicit
canonical carrier that undergoes the same delivery process. Sending an
opening with a risky inclusion chance and taking FALSE by a certain
message-free timeout do not satisfy this assumption. The source may remain
abstract; the compiler must implement the logical distinction through the
declared uniform interface.

**Mechanical service projection.** Strip all logical payloads, private
binding meanings, source choices and source chance outcomes from physical
state and recall. Retain the public stage, delivery commands, clocks, attempt
counts, packet identifiers, operational receipts and service observations.
Call this the mechanical state and each owner's remembered mechanical view W.
There is a fixed finite delivery game on these states and views, with perfect
recall and stable menus. Menus depend on W and the stage, never on a private
logical value or selected source action.

Its initial service state is independent of source initial parameters. Every
mechanical transition and observation kernel depends only on preceding
mechanical state, public stage and mechanical controls. Hidden persistent
service state and correlated private operational observations are allowed.
Remove structurally zero-probability mechanical chance edges from the legal
tree as for source chance. Probability-zero or probability-one service laws
are allowed; rare off-path player choices are retained.
The whole mechanical process ignores **all** logical data, including values
that have already become public. Every admissible canonical source packet
has exactly this projected law at every compatible physical history. This
is a pointwise transition/coupling property, not an assertion about honest
initialized averages. Source-dependent packet size, validation cost, priority
or load would require a new analysis.

Full target observations must also separate coherently. At a genuine source
decision the complete input is recoverable original source information I
plus W and fields already fixed by I. At a delivery decision it is a source
view S plus W; S retains original own recall, the selected current action
when known, and any current payload known to the delivery owner. Equality
of S fixes every logical field exposed in that view. Under a mechanical
policy ignoring S, the complete mechanical-prefix law and its operational
views are identical for every logical prefix. Observation noise belongs
to the mechanical state or jointly source-independent seeds, not to a
source-dependent receipt factor.

For a serial opaque commit-reveal implementation, the useful induction is
concrete: canonical commitments disclose no binding meaning; the current
opening value is known to its owner; earlier successful openings are
source-public before the next logical decision. Pending plaintext remains
readable during forced waits. Absorbing failure ends the game before another
logical choice. Full-envelope and own-recall coupling, not packet invisibility,
must establish the stated observation decomposition. The
[native observation argument](native-observation-criterion.md) supplies its
protected serial counterpart, not this complete risky-control adapter.

**Utility and costs.** On whole-game completion, net utility is exactly the
source u_i. On every physical abort, player i receives the same constant C_i,
independent of who failed, when, which packet was pending, selected private
value, delivery mode, previous source choices or any later policy. Suppose

\[
C_i\le\min_h u_i(h).                            \tag{1}
\]

The minimum ranges over all legal source terminals, including off-path ones.
Actual settlement must realize this rule. No successful-send fees, mode
costs, financing/time preferences, balance-dependent menus or additional
rewards are modeled. Funding is a prior condition, not a strategic choice;
refund or payout feasibility is not proved. Producer incentives, coalitions,
participation and outside markets/channels are excluded. Canonical hiding and
authenticity are idealized and still require a concrete metadata refinement.

A physical abort is not itself a transaction that can collect money during
a permanent chain halt. A missed-publication deadline needs a separate funded
settlement guarantee within the declared physical model. Permanent halted-chain
faults that prevent this settlement are excluded unless an independent
mechanism realizes the specified utilities. Merely assuming an eventual
recovery does not establish the finite settlement bound used by this game.

Condition (1) is stronger than a neutral refund when source net payoffs can
be negative. An affine normalization of utility cannot change the ordering
between a refund and a losing source payoff. The result must not be read as
justifying a cost-free abort option for a player who prefers cancelling a
loss. Failure utility that charges only the owner or changes with its attempt
also lies outside this theorem.

For example, an optional execution layer could settle all participants' funded
escrow to the same declared low outcome after any missed stage, while reserving
ordinary source payoffs for completed execution. That can impose losses on
innocent participants as well as the failed stage's owner. It is a mathematical
candidate for fail-stop accounting, not current settlement semantics or a
claim about a deployed chain. A backend certificate must separately justify
its value-blind controls, admission resources and executable settlement.

## Paper theorem and its quantifiers

Fix the source, canonical interface, abort utilities and a class of mechanical
services before choosing a source equilibrium. For each fully specified
service, there is a mechanical delivery policy pi_star, depending on that
service but not on the selected source SE, such that every source SE
(sigma,mu) has a target SE with:

- logical choices copied from sigma;
- delivery choices pi_star using only stage and remembered mechanical view;
- source beliefs projected to mu at corresponding genuine decisions;
- exact joint law of initialized source parameters and source terminals
  conditional on whole-game completion, whenever completion has positive
  probability.

Let p be its initialized probability of completion. The unconditional target
law is p Q_sigma + (1-p) A_sigma, where Q_sigma is the source terminal law
and A_sigma is the actual distinct abort branch. On completion source
utilities are exact; on abort they are C_i. No exact unconditional logical-law
preservation is claimed.

Moreover, p is at least the completion probability of every admissible
mechanical fallback policy. Thus a public fallback with guaranteed completion
probability p_0 supplies p>=p_0. The policy achieving this conclusion is
selected for the actual service. A common complete policy based only on broad
miner properties requires another dominance or Bayesian argument.

This is exact forward SE with delivery-conditioned source law, not approximate
SE or reflection of all target equilibria. The proof is written mathematics;
no native raw adapter or Lean theorem is asserted.

## Optimize whole-game completion before selecting the source SE

Form the finite mechanical delivery game by erasing the source choices and
values, leaving their fixed stage positions and the actual delivery controls.
It has the same physical completion/abort event. Give every delivery agent
payoff one if the **entire H-stage game** completes and zero otherwise.
This is a proof game, not a claim that modeled players literally have that
utility. The actual utility comparison will be proved below.

Let m_W be the action count at mechanical information W, and let m_max be its
finite maximum, using one if there are no decisions. For delta>0 small enough,
restrict every available mechanical action probability to be at least delta.
This is a nonempty compact product of simplexes. The finite completion
probability p(pi) is continuous, so choose a global maximizer pi_delta.
All legal information sets have positive reach under pi_delta.

Fix W and change only its current lottery, leaving every other delivery
lottery unchanged. Perfect recall makes W an antichain: it is not visited
twice on a path, and its own action cannot affect its preceding reach mass.
Let r_delta(W)>0 be that mass and q_delta(W,a) the conditional probability
of whole-game completion after action a and prescribed pi_delta continuation,
using the actual Bayesian mechanical belief at W. The global change is

\[
r_\delta(W)\left[
 \sum_a\eta(a)q_\delta(W,a)
 -\sum_a\pi_\delta(a\mid W)q_\delta(W,a)\right].
\]

Global maximization makes this nonpositive for every delta-constrained eta.
Divide by r_delta(W) before taking a limit. For any pure action a, use the
constrained lottery giving it 1-(m_W-1)delta and giving every other action
delta. Since q_delta lies in [0,1], this gives

\[
q_\delta(W,a)
 -\sum_b\pi_\delta(b\mid W)q_\delta(W,b)
 \le(m_W-1)\delta.                              \tag{2}
\]

This conditional bound remains valid when W becomes arbitrarily rare.
Initialized near-optimal completion alone would not establish it.

Choose delta_n tending to zero and one common compact subsequence of
pi_delta_n and all their full Bayesian mechanical beliefs. Denote the limits
by pi_star and rho_star. The original fully mixed sequence gives consistency.
Finite continuation probabilities are continuous in full beliefs and
behavioral policies. Passing (2) to the limit proves every local completion
comparison. Perfect recall gives sequential rationality in the proof game.

Select this convergent mechanical sequence and its limit before choosing a
source SE. It is the same for every source assessment for this service.
Conditional completion can be zero at some off-path sites; then its local
objective may be tied. No posterior or unreachable action is discarded.

Global rather than merely equilibrium selection also supplies the promised
completion bound. Given any fallback pi_f, define its fully mixed approximation

\[
\pi_f^\delta(a\mid W)
=(1-m_W\delta)\pi_f(a\mid W)+\delta.
\]

It lies in the constrained policy space and tends to pi_f. Hence
p(pi_delta)>=p(pi_f^delta). Continuity yields

\[
p(\pi_\star)\ge p(\pi_f).                       \tag{3}
\]

A cooperative proof game can have poor coordination equilibria. Arbitrarily
selecting one such equilibrium would not establish (3); the constrained
global maximization is why this particular selection does.

## One combined consistency sequence in the actual game

For a selected source SE, choose its fully mixed consistency sequence sigma_n
with Bayesian source beliefs mu_n tending to mu. Combine it with the already
chosen pi_delta_n. Use sigma_n at each genuine source choice, pi_delta_n at
each actual delivery copy with mechanical input W, and silence at singleton
physical sites. Every actual available player action is positive.

Let g be the logical prefix, including any currently selected but not yet
delivered action. Let omega be the mechanical prefix, including hidden
service state, actual operational chance choices and observation seeds.
The source-independent projection and copied/blind policies imply, by
multiplying actual finite transition kernels,

\[
w'_n(g,\omega)=w_n(g)Q_n(\omega).               \tag{4}
\]

Q_n is a subprobability prefix law of the actual mechanical process under
pi_delta_n; paths that already abort are absent. It can depend on n. It is
not a normalized law obtained by conditioning on future success. The source
weights contain sigma_n and the unchanged source chance factors; Q_n contains
only mechanical factors. No later logical chance outcome is fixed during
this prefix replay.

At a genuine decision J=(I,W), Bayes' rule cancels the common operational
likelihood and projects the source marginal to mu_n at I. Conditional on
J, the hidden logical prefix and mechanical prefix have a product belief;
the mechanical factor is the Bayesian belief of the proof game at W.
At a delivery decision J=(S,W), the same cancellation gives a logical
belief conditional on S times that **same** mechanical belief at W.
S may include the newly selected action or its authentic fixed payload.
This logical marginal need not be a source belief at a genuine original
decision: take its actual Bayesian limit, rather than calling it mu.

Take one common compact subsequence of all additional full target beliefs
at delivery and singleton sites. It preserves sigma, mu, pi_star and
rho_star, since those limits have already been selected. Formula (4) gives
one globally consistent target assessment. In particular a different
convenient posterior has not been chosen independently at each late send,
retry or waiting site.

## Source choices and delivery choices are both rational

Only local one-shot changes followed by the prescribed continuation are
needed. Finite perfect recall and consistency then cover every whole
adaptive target policy. The following factorizations are not asserted for
arbitrary future policies that change source decisions using service signals.

**Genuine source choices.** At J=(I,W), change its current source lottery to
eta. Delivery still uses the blind pi_star and future logical choices use
sigma. From every full current mechanical state the remaining whole-game
completion probability is independent of eta and the hidden logical prefix.
Abort always pays C_i. Product beliefs therefore give

\[
V_i^{\rm target}(J;\eta)
 =C_i+q_J\bigl[V_i^{\rm source}(I;\eta)-C_i\bigr]. \tag{5}
\]

Here q_J>=0 is the conditional probability of all remaining stages completing,
not merely the next packet arriving. Source sequential rationality under mu
makes every gain relative to sigma(I) nonpositive. If q_J=0 all source
lotteries are tied, which still permits copying.

**Delivery choices.** Fix a delivery information set J=(S,W). From a logical
prefix g in S, let V_i(g) be source utility conditional on this current
selected action completing, followed by sigma. It lies between the minimum
and maximum legal source terminal utility. A current delivery action changes
only the mechanical probability of whole-game completion; upon completion
the logical continuation law is unchanged, and on any failure the utility
is C_i. Product beliefs give the gain from action a over pi_star as

\[
\left[\mathbb E(V_i(g)\mid S)-C_i\right]
\left[q_\star(W,a)-q_\star(W,\pi_\star)\right]. \tag{6}
\]

The first bracket is nonnegative by (1). The second is nonpositive by
the mechanical local comparison proved from (2). Thus every delivery
action is rational against its actual full belief, even though its owner
knows a private committed value or has a different source continuation
value than another type.

Equivalently, before taking limits, the actual conditional delivery regret
is at most (U_i-C_i)(m_W-1)delta_n, where U_i is the finite maximum source
utility. This uses the proxy's actual operational posterior and the true
logical posterior, not an arbitrary belief supplied to force Send. It tends
to zero; continuation-value continuity gives (6).

Singleton physical sites are trivially rational. All genuine and delivery
comparisons now hold in one consistent assessment. The finite
one-shot-deviation principle completes the exact target SE proof. A player
can adapt future retries, waits and source choices to all its observations;
that deviation class has not been restricted to mechanically blind policies.
Blindness describes the constructed delivery equilibrium, while the local
comparisons cover all available mechanical actions at every rich input.

### Utility variant with immutable initial parameters

The same proof permits C_i(x), where x is an immutable initial source
parameter, if **every** physical abort still pays that same C_i(x), independent
of cause, timing, controller and route, and

\[
C_i(x)\le u_i(h)
\quad\hbox{for every legal source terminal h with initial parameter x}.
\]

At a genuine source decision the expected C_i(x) is unchanged by its current
lottery, so the gain in (5) is still q_J times the source gain. At a control
decision (6) becomes the mechanical completion gain multiplied by
E[V_i(g)-C_i(x(g))|S], which is nonnegative statewise. Factorization,
consistency, the delivered law and fallback bound are unchanged. Expected
abort utility now uses the fixed initial source distribution. A strategically
chosen binding value is not an immutable initial parameter for this purpose.
Actual settlement must implement the variant as well. Independent mathematical
review accepted this extension.

## Delivered law and a finite whole-game success guarantee

Under sigma and pi_star, (4) at complete terminal paths gives

\[
\Pr(h,\mathrm{Complete})=p\,w_\sigma(h),
\qquad p=p(\pi_\star).
\]

All initialized source parameters are retained in h. The mechanical process
is independent of the entire logical path, including source-public chance
outcomes. For p>0, conditioning gives exactly the source joint law, without
resampling an input or pretending that an unfinished abort is a terminal
source execution. Expected utility is

\[
\mathbb E U_i=p\,\mathbb E_\sigma u_i+(1-p)C_i.
\]

With a distinct abort branch, total variation from the source terminal law
on their common enlarged readout space is exactly 1-p. A coarser fixed
fallback readout has error at most 1-p.

Suppose a public mechanical fallback can finish source stage s with
probability at least b_s>0 after every compatible mechanical past on that
fallback, conditional on all earlier stages succeeding. This is an actual
policy guarantee, uniform in logical choices and permitted load. Then finite
conditional multiplication gives

\[
p(\pi_f)\ge\prod_{s=1}^H b_s=:p_0,
\qquad
p(\pi_\star)\ge p_0,
\qquad
1-p\le1-\prod_s b_s\le\sum_s(1-b_s).          \tag{7}
\]

No independence between stages is used. No lower success bound is imposed
on every alternative delivery action; some Wait or Retry choices may fail
surely. Those choices are covered by the proxy's local optimization.
The fallback bound supplies attainable initialization reliability, while
the conditional comparisons supply off-path rationality. Neither is a
substitute for the other. A fixed logical horizon supplies a finite product
only when the physical per-stage control process is itself bounded.

## A common delivery policy under persistent monotone opportunities

There is a useful sufficient service class in which the delivery policy need
not know the selected transition law. Retain all previous source, observation,
utility and cost assumptions, and impose the following extra physical
properties on every realized finite absolute-time opportunity stream.

- Jobs form the fixed serial sequence. At most one current canonical job is
  active; sending the same job again is idempotent.
- Once submitted, a job remains eligible until inclusion or its fixed absolute
  deadline. There is no eviction, cancellation penalty, validity expiration
  before that deadline, fee change or loss of priority from submitting early.
- Earlier stage readiness gives a superset of that job's admissible submission
  callbacks and inclusion opportunities. An earlier first submission preserves
  every later eligibility opportunity available after a later submission.
- Opportunities and future callback availability are fixed by the exogenous
  stream. Submitting or completing early does not remove a later opportunity,
  change a later deadline, consume future reserved capacity, or disadvantage
  the next job. Next-stage readiness is monotone in the prior completion time.

At a realized inclusion opportunity, the currently eligible job is actually
delivered; an opportunity is not merely feasible space awaiting another
submission-dependent selection or tie-breaking lottery. Independent background
traffic can be folded into unavailable slots only if these same monotonicity
properties remain valid.

The opportunity stream can be correlated, unknown or privately observed, and
some paths can lack enough opportunities. These monotonicity properties must
hold from every legal continuation state, including a partly pending current
job. They are not implied by miner honesty or by opportunities being random.

Define Greedy to submit the current fixed job at its first eligible callback
and keep it pending, rebroadcasting the identical job only where that is
idempotent. It advances to the next job as soon as its predecessor succeeds.
Every required logical choice is made in the original menu once its fixed
stage is available; it uses the copied source policy.

**Paper common-policy corollary.** For this service class, Greedy together with
each selected source SE is a target SE with consistent source-projecting
beliefs. The same complete delivery rule works for every service law in the
class, without computing its inclusion probabilities. Its initialization
completion probability is at least any admissible fallback's probability,
and the delivered source law and error bounds above apply.

**Pathwise proof.** Couple Greedy and an arbitrary alternative to the same
exogenous stream, starting from the same complete legal mechanical history.
For the current job, an earlier ready time permits every later callback that
the alternative can use. Greedy's first submission is therefore no later.
Persistence and eligibility monotonicity preserve the alternative's eventual
inclusion opportunity for Greedy, so Greedy's completion time is no later
whenever the alternative completes that job. This includes an already pending
job: retaining it cannot lose a later opportunity by idempotence/persistence.

Induct over subsequent jobs. Earlier predecessor completion makes Greedy's
next job ready no later; callbacks, deadlines and later opportunities satisfy
the same monotonicity. Every successful alternative completion time is an
upper bound on the corresponding Greedy time. Thus if the alternative finishes
all jobs by their deadlines, Greedy does too. If a job misses its deadline
under Greedy, no alternative with a later ready/submission time could have
completed the remaining serial game on that stream. Whole-game completion
under Greedy pathwise dominates every alternative, from every legal history.

**Consistency and rationality proof.** Fully mix the original source sequence
with source-blind mechanical perturbations of Greedy, then use the product
construction (4) and one common compact belief subsequence. This gives the
same source projections and globally consistent full beliefs. At any delivery
site and every compatible hidden state, Greedy continuation maximizes
whole-game completion on every future stream. It consequently satisfies the
mechanical local comparison under every possible belief, including the
limiting off-path beliefs. Finite continuation-value continuity applies as
the perturbations vanish. Equations (5) and (6) give source and delivery
rationality in the limiting target assessment. The initialized pathwise
domination gives the fallback bound directly. No exact service probability
or service-specific optimal-control computation is needed. QED.

This is another conditional candidate, not a deployed-chain claim. Competing
jobs, fee-dependent priority, mempool eviction, deadline resets, limits on
earlier readiness, nonce/validity changes, censorship reacting to submission,
or capacity feedback can break the time induction. Ordinary rebroadcast being
idempotent does not by itself establish all of the conditions. Source and
service independence still matter for the source belief and delivered-law
conclusions; pathwise delivery dominance alone does not prove those.

## The knowledge and realism boundaries

A mechanically optimal pi_star can depend on the service's actual transition
kernel and private operational signals. This theorem is pointwise in that
kernel, with a common source-copy policy. It does not infer a single delivery
policy that is sequentially optimal for all otherwise unknown miners.

For a simple strict contrast, suppose one delivery choice is Send or Wait.
One admissible service completes surely after Send and fails after Wait;
another completes surely after Wait and fails after Send. Both ignore all
source data. With source utility one and abort zero, their unique rational
delivery choices differ. A common broad-property policy need not be an SE
for both pointwise games. A genuinely universally dominating delivery rule,
or a specified prior over the two services, is a different hypothesis.

An independent hidden persistent service with a specified common prior can
be folded into the mechanical Nature state. Maximizing Bayesian whole-game
completion then gives the same proof. Private service learning updates only
the mechanical posterior, and does not tilt source beliefs. Source-correlated
service, strategic producers, or unspecified ambiguity need other models.
Noncollusion alone supplies none of these kernel or belief properties.

The abort ordering is also material. With one mandatory source terminal
paying its delivery owner zero, Send completing surely and Wait failing
surely, an abort payoff one makes Wait strictly preferable. No target SE
completes, despite the existence of a perfectly reliable fallback. This
refutes the positive-delivery conclusion if that ordering is removed; a
zero-completion conditional law is simply undefined.

**Owner-only forfeit can shift failure to another stage.** Consider two
mandatory source stages, with forced terminal utilities (-1,1). Player 1
chooses its stage-1 delivery plan: Send now or Wait and send later. Both
deliver its own job surely. After Send, player 2's sole stage-2 delivery action
completes surely. After Wait, its sole action fails surely. A physical failure
pays -D to its stage owner and zero to the other player, with D>1. Such
settlement is identity-dependent, not the common C_i rule. Player 1 gets -1
from Send and zero from Wait, so Wait is uniquely rational and whole-game
completion has probability zero. The all-Send fallback completes surely.
Its own eventual successful admission is why increasing its own failure
forfeit does not deter the timing plan. This is an abstract finite service,
not a native embedding.

The same issue can occur even if every abort payoff is no higher than the
corresponding source utility. Use source utilities (0,1), D>1 and a parameter
1/D<epsilon<1. Stage-1 Send fails with probability epsilon; otherwise both
stages complete surely. Stage-1 Wait succeeds surely but makes player 2's sole
stage-2 action fail surely. For each player, its own-stage failure pays -D;
the other stage's failure pays -1. Player 1 receives -epsilon D after Send
and -1 after Wait, so Wait is uniquely optimal. A stage-1 Never alternative,
if included, pays -D and is worse. No target SE completes, while the Send
fallback has positive success 1-epsilon. All abort utilities are below the
source utilities; what fails is their constancy across causes. For large D,
epsilon just above 1/D makes the fallback arbitrarily reliable. The service
reads only the chosen delivery plan, with no source secret, payload or fee
dependence. Stage-2 menus are explicitly singleton, so another late action
cannot restore delivery in this comparison.

In this stronger version, the settlement is a common abort loss of one plus
an additional owner-specific forfeit D-1. The previous zero-refund example
has literally owner-only loss and also violates the low-abort ordering.

If different source actions have different survival laws, even logical
choice preservation can fail. A source choice A paying one is uniquely
preferred to B paying 3/4. If A's canonical transport succeeds with
probability 1/10, B's succeeds surely and abort pays zero, the target uniquely
chooses B. This applies to an admitted source decline that bypasses transport,
as well as to a value-dependent priority rule. The common transport and
value-blind conditions are distinct from cryptographic hiding.

This result permits visible pending packets and multiple delivery callbacks,
but requires ordinary submission, retries and waits to have one coherent
data-independent mechanical semantics. Real inclusion can depend on fees,
encoded length, execution cost, validity, MEV, competing transactions and
source-dependent load. Source choices can also affect timeout settlement.
Those effects are not erased merely by choosing a mechanical projection.
An implementation must prove its pointwise coupling and utility conditions
or explicitly weaken the target. The examples establish boundaries of these
comparisons, not a theorem that every weakening fails in every game.

## Relationship to the concrete runtime and result card

For the native runtime, opacity, readiness, serial barriers, canonical packet
construction and full recall are useful ingredients. The missing adapters
are the full value-blind control quotient, uniform source-action transport,
unchanged future logical chance, actual abort settlement and finite physical
menus. Native FALSE by silence/timeout, owner-specific forfeits, late fresh
binding selection and unrestricted raw packets are particularly different
from this interface. No checked result or adopted checklist is replaced.

- **Result:** Paper exact target SE for every finite fixed-stage source SE,
  using a source-blind delivery policy maximizing whole-game completion.
  Multiple finite Send/Wait/Retry callbacks and genuine delivery risk are
  included; sure protected inclusion is unnecessary.
- **Source and observations:** Correlated retained secrets, once-selected
  canonical logical actions and unchanged menus/chance. Original source
  information plus mechanically independent full recall must describe every
  genuine choice; readable pending plaintext is allowed at forced waits.
- **Utility requirement:** Completion pays the source utility, every physical
  abort the same C_i no greater than every legal source payoff. Fees, mode
  costs, capital timing, participation, producer incentives, external markets
  and coalitions are excluded. Actual low abort settlement is an assumption.
- **Output and quantifiers:** Interface and failure payoffs first; service
  next; delivery policy selected for that service before the source SE. Exact
  source law conditional on completion, explicit unconditional abort mixture,
  and at least the public fallback's whole-game success guarantee.
- **Knowledge:** Optimal delivery is service-specific unless a universal
  dominating policy is separately proved. The persistent monotone-opportunity
  corollary supplies one common Greedy policy under its stronger all-history
  assumptions. Independent hidden service with a common prior also fits;
  noncollusion alone does not specify optimal control.
- **Status:** Independent mathematical review accepted the global consistency,
  local comparison, delivery-law, fallback-bound and immutable-parameter
  arguments, the common Greedy corollary and both failure-identity
  counterexamples. No Lean theorem or full native raw adapter is claimed.
