# Serial immutable openings with one late opportunity

Analysis by Codex. This note proves a finite-game preservation theorem for
several disclosure phases with retained correlated private information. It is
a proposed restricted interface, not the adopted VegasCore runtime. Pending
plaintext remains readable. The restriction concerns when another genuine
choice can occur, and which value a late opening can transmit.

The results are paper theorems. They permit accepted late openings without
requiring a positive lower bound on their failure probability. The first uses
a public lower bound on success, one late callback per opening, protected
binding decisions, and a publicly marked failure continuation. A second
replaces the success bound with publicly reconstructible conditional success
odds. General raw packets, repeated callbacks and late binding choices are
outside both theorems.

## Source and physical interface

**Source game.** Fix a finite perfect-recall game generated by a fixed public
serial instruction sequence. Owners and the current instruction are public.
Nature can initialize correlated private types and bindings. Later chance
instructions draw public source outcomes from the specified source-public
context. Remove zero-probability chance edges from the legal tree. At a binding
instruction, its owner chooses a value from its entire legal finite source
menu; the value is bound successfully and remains private. An opening
instruction has a single mandatory action: publish the immutable bound value.
The owner knows that value and its opening material. Every legal opening is
effective, including after a different lawful binding choice. The source
terminal utilities satisfy 0 <= u_i <= R_i.

Several earlier secrets can remain private across later decisions. The game
need not reset types between phases. It does not offer lawful withholding:
adding a punishment to an existing withholding choice would change its source
utility. This theorem must therefore not be advertised as preservation for
arbitrary TRUE/FALSE source programs.

**Physical interface.** Start from fixed public handles and an empty network.
Every binding choice uses a canonical opaque envelope and protected admission.
Its private registration can change its owner's private catalogue but changes
no public packet, public receipt or foreign observation. Handles and envelope
sizes depend on the public instruction history, not the private value. Binding
menus cover all source values; no retry, alternative encoding, additional
candidate preparation or late binding decision is available.

At a mandatory opening, its owner chooses either:

- **Open:** emit its canonical authenticated fixed-value opening at a protected
  opportunity. It is surely accepted and advances the source instruction.
- **Defer:** wait until exactly one later owned callback. There the owner can
  **Send** the same immutable opening, or **Never** send it. Send succeeds with
  conditional probability at least a declared q_min > 0. Never fails surely.

There are no intervening source choices before this opening resolves. All
other physical response sites offer only silence. Physical waiting and packet
observation are retained as bounded finite chance processing. On success,
the value is published and the next source instruction becomes available. The
accepted late opening has no additional deduction. On the first failed Send
or Never, every player observes a sticky failure marker before any further
strategic choice. Execution then enters a finite perfect-recall failure
continuation and never returns to a decision information set that also
contains a no-failure history. The continuation can contain arbitrary actions
and retained secrets; it need not prescribe honest behavior.

**Observations and primitive service inputs.** Public processing kernels may
inspect all pending packets, prior envelopes, identities, receipts, clocks
and their complete public history. After Send, that includes the opening's
plaintext. They may depend on the newly emitted immutable value. They do not
inspect unrevealed private binding meanings or recipients' private passive
samples. Passive sampling kernels can inspect the actual pending packet list
and produce correlated remembered private samples. All player policies can
use their entire own action and observation recall.

Equal service inputs have equal transition laws. Apart from canonical source
responses, processing changes only physical state until instruction resolution.
On every complete physical history, successful source transitions, original
source observations and subsequent logical chance kernels have precisely the
source law. The service cannot predict a source chance draw by correlating it
with earlier physical randomness. Source-public outcomes are not resampled.
The interface terminates within its finite bound on all legal histories.

The success lower bound holds conditionally on every legal no-failure history
at Send, not just initialized prescribed play. Success can be certain. No
upper cap below one, independence between phases, uniform delay distribution,
or globally payload-blind scheduler is imposed. The public processing inputs
above, serial frontier and canonical envelopes provide the information
argument; a bare inclusion probability does not.

**Costs and accountability.** On every terminal history without a publication
failure, net utility equals the source utility. On a failure continuation,
player i's gross utility is in [0,R_i] and its nonnegative total deductions
are at most

\[
M_i=N_iD_i+K_i.
\]

Here N_i is a declared nonnegative integer, positive for players with an
opening, D_i is a failure forfeit, and K_i >= 0 bounds all additional
deductions. A first failure
caused by i collects at least D_i from i, regardless of subsequent policies.
These are actual settlement bounds, not obligations to pay later. All amounts
are available before play. Collected amounts go outside the modeled players,
or equivalent gross-utility bounds already account for any redistribution.

No extra fees, financing costs, delay preferences, balance-dependent menus,
participation choices, outside trades or producer incentives are modeled.
Coalitions and additional communication channels are excluded. Cryptographic
hiding and authenticity are idealized canonical operations; a computational
refinement and metadata analysis are separate obligations. Failure penalties
are off path relative to this mandatory-opening source. Capital feasibility
does not follow from the finite bounds.

## Paper preservation theorem

Fix the public source/interface descriptor, R_i, N_i, K_i and q_min before
selecting the service or a source equilibrium. For every player with an
opening, suppose

\[
D_i>R_i,
\qquad
D_i\bigl[1-N_i(1-q_{\min})\bigr]
  > R_i+(1-q_{\min})K_i.                       \tag{1}
\]

For every service satisfying the primitive interface and every source SE,
there is a target SE with the same joint law of initialized types, erased
source terminal histories and realized source utilities. Its prescribed
strategy copies the source binding choices, chooses protected Open at every
opening, and chooses Send at every off-path late callback. Failure
continuations are completed with rational consistent policies. No deduction
occurs on initialized play.

This is exact forward implementation, not reflection of every target
equilibrium. The copied policy before failure is common to the service class.
Beliefs and failure-continuation policies may depend on the service. An unknown
persistent service needs its own Bayesian game and joint information-channel
argument; pointwise existence is not a prior-free strategy guarantee.

The proof below supplies global consistency and all local rationality
comparisons. The native observation and audit adapters remain separate: this
is not a theorem for the full native response menu.

## Successful-prefix coupling

Let (sigma,mu) be the selected source SE, and choose its one fully mixed
consistency sequence sigma_n, with Bayesian beliefs mu_n converging to mu.
Choose epsilon_n in (0,1) tending to zero. At every protected opening lottery,
use Defer with probability epsilon_n. At every late callback, use Never with
probability epsilon_n. These probabilities are the same for every type and
remembered physical input. Source binding choices use sigma_n at the recovered
source information, ignoring auxiliary records. Every no-failure player menu
is then fully mixed.

For a source prefix h and a physical prefix omega before failure, replay the
physical kernels with only the past source actions and logical chance outcomes
in h externally fixed. Include the epsilon_n transport lotteries. Stop at the
specified physical decision; discard paths that already fail or do not reach
that decision. Call the resulting mass L_n(h,omega). It is a
**subprobability** kernel: some deferred paths fail. It is not a normalized
law obtained by conditioning on successful delivery. Its dependence on n is
essential.

Multiplication of actual finite transition probabilities gives

\[
w'_n(h,\omega)=w_n(h)L_n(h,\omega),             \tag{2}
\]

where w_n(h) is the source reach weight under sigma_n. The source-action and
logical-chance factors contribute w_n. The physical kernels and transport
lotteries contribute L_n. No future source outcome is fixed in this replay.

For any genuine binding decision, protected opening decision or late
callback owned by i, let I be its corresponding source information set and
z its full remembered physical record. In the latter two cases I is the
source's singleton-action opening information set. Stage labels and full own
recall recover I; the protected opportunity and late callback are distinct
target decisions.

**Coupling lemma.** If h and h' have the same source information I for i,
their successful-prefix replays have the same joint subprobability marginal
of i's full physical record and the complete public service history. In
particular, for every z,

\[
\ell_{n,I}(z)
 :=\sum_{\omega:J_i(h,\omega)=(I,z)}L_n(h,\omega)
\]

does not depend on the choice of h in I.

**Proof.** Equal source information supplies the current public instruction,
the entire previous source-public history, and i's earlier own information
and choices. Couple physical commands and samples whenever their inputs
agree. Initially public handles, network and i's own state agree. Foreign
initial private meanings can differ.

A prior binding emits the same canonical opaque envelope in both replays.
Its public preparation effect is the same. Protected inclusion produces the
same public receipt. The instruction count fixes the same fresh handle; no
secret-dependent fallback search occurs. If i owns the binding, its value,
private registration and actual remembered action agree by source recall.
Foreign private registrations need not agree.

Every prior successfully resolved opening has a value in the current
source-public history, so it emits the same value, evidence and envelope in
both replays. The type-independent transport lotteries can be coupled. Their
protected/deferred routes, waits, pending payloads, resolution commands and
public receipt histories agree, including their probabilities. Failed routes
discard the same paired mass. At a current owner's late callback, the fixed
value is already in that owner's source information, although it need not
yet be public.

Every logical public chance outcome in the fixed prefix agrees, and its
probability has already been factored into w_n. Surrounding physical
processing has equal public inputs. No later draw is conditioned upon.
At an activation, equal pending packets give equal passive-sample laws.
Couple the actual samples; i's sampled packets and full remembered input
agree. Private samples do not alter subsequent service inputs. The same
argument preserves complete public service recall, not merely its latest
receipt or i's marginal view.

Serial dependencies ensure that no genuine source decision is reached before
the preceding opening either publishes or enters the marked failure tail.
Thus every plaintext that influenced processing before a later no-failure
choice is fixed by the source-public prefix for that choice. Correlated
unrevealed meanings remain absent from all public inputs. Induction through
the bounded physical replay proves the joint marginal equality. QED.

This is the same useful frontier as the
[native serial observation argument](native-observation-criterion.md), now
applied to successful paths with transport trembles. Readable pending packets
are included. If a private fee, secret-dependent length, early alternative
opening, or timing-dependent source menu enters those inputs, this induction
must be reproved and may fail.

At a legal target decision J=(I,z), ell_{n,I}(z)>0. Bayes' rule applied to (2)
therefore gives, for every n,

\[
\Pr_n(h\mid J)=
\frac{w_n(h)\ell_{n,I}(z)}{
       \sum_{g\in I}w_n(g)\ell_{n,I}(z)}
=\mu_{n,i}(h\mid I).                            \tag{3}
\]

All legal no-failure histories have positive weight under the mixed profile.
No limiting zero denominator is used in (3). Full target beliefs also include
the conditional physical-record kernel derived from L_n. That kernel can
depend on n and the probability of J can vanish. A common compact subsequence
of the finite target belief simplexes gives convergent full beliefs while
their source projection still converges to mu. Newly owned waiting sites
have a singleton menu; their full beliefs are included in that same
subsequence and need no asserted source-belief formula.

## Completing every failure continuation with the actual entry prior

The first-failure histories form a cutset: every failure history extends
exactly one first entry and no no-failure history extends an entry. Collect
all these complete entry histories E, including their source histories,
public physical records, private samples and every player's prior recall.
The no-failure mixed profile induces a positive mass p_n(e) for each legal
e in E. Normalize the masses to an actual entry prior nu_n. No choice in a
failure tail affects these masses.

If E is empty, use the successful replay directly and omit this continuation
construction. Otherwise all its legal entry masses are positive because
source and transport menus are fully mixed and every remaining legal chance
edge has positive probability.

Make a virtual finite continuation game: Nature selects e with prior nu_n,
each player receives exactly its original remembered information at e, and
play follows the actual failure tail above e. Its information sets are the
original target failure information sets. Entry prefixes are included in the
hidden state and private signals, so this construction preserves earlier own
action recall; it does not replace the entry with only the public failure
label. The sticky marker ensures that no information set in this game also
contains a no-failure history. All first entries can be combined in one such
game; separate public labels are unnecessary.

More explicitly, equal original recall at any later failure information set
fixes the same earlier owned information labels and actions. Each genuine
owned label is either entirely before the cutset or entirely after it, because
failure and no-failure information sets are disjoint. Deleting the entry
prefixes therefore leaves the same ordered sequence of post-entry own labels
and actions on both histories. The truncated continuation has perfect recall,
even though different entries occurred at different original depths.

For each n, choose an SE (tau^n,eta^n) of this finite perfect-recall
continuation game. Choose a fully mixed consistency witness, for this
**same** nu_n, whose full strategy and all full Bayesian beliefs are within
1/n of (tau^n,eta^n). Such a witness exists by that SE's consistency. Denote its
strategy by hat_tau_n. Splice hat_tau_n into the no-failure mixed profile.
The resulting global strategy is fully mixed.

At every failure information set, global Bayes beliefs are exactly those of
the virtual game under nu_n and hat_tau_n: the common total entry probability
cancels, and no no-failure history belongs to that set. This identity uses
the actual mixed entry prior for each n, even when different entry masses
vanish at different rates. It does not assume an arbitrary convenient
off-path belief or interchange a zero-probability limit with Bayes' rule.

Choose one common compact subsequence of all global strategies, all global
beliefs, and (tau^n,eta^n). The witnesses and their SE assessments have the
same limits at failure sites. Finite continuation expected utilities are
continuous functions of the full beliefs and behavioral policies. Every
local rationality inequality of (tau^n,eta^n) therefore passes to their common
limit. The global limiting assessment is sequentially rational at all failure
sites and is consistent there by the spliced mixed sequence. At no-failure
sites its projected beliefs are mu by (3).

Only a standard finite perfect-recall SE existence fact is used above. It can
also be obtained directly for each positive entry prior: constrain every
action probability to be at least a small delta, find an equilibrium of the
finite agent game, and take a common limit as delta tends to zero. Positive
reach turns each agent's ex ante comparison into its conditional local
comparison. Finite continuation-value continuity gives local optimality in
the limit, Bayesian beliefs give consistency, and perfect recall supplies
the finite one-shot-deviation equivalence.

## Rationality before the first failure

In the limiting strategy, every future transport uses protected Open and
every source decision copies sigma. From any complete no-failure physical
history above source history h, that continuation erases to the source
continuation from h. Its value S_i(h) lies in [0,R_i]. This identity holds
conditional on the complete physical history, rather than only after
averaging the initialized outcome law.

**A genuine source binding choice.** Fix one target binding information set
and change its value lottery once, keeping prescribed continuation. Every
legal value is protected and binds successfully. Erasure gives the same
source continuation for this lottery. Averaging over the projected belief
mu makes its expected gain nonpositive by source sequential rationality.
Full remembered auxiliary data do not add another term to the payoff.

**The protected opening decision.** Conditional on a hidden complete history,
Open yields S_i(h). Defer followed by the prescribed Send yields S_i(h) on
success in conditional expectation, and at most R_i-D_i on failure.
Successful physical records can differ, but subsequent copied source
policies ignore them and the logical chance law remains unchanged on each
record. Write omega for the current full physical record and let q(h,omega)
be the aggregate conditional Send success probability, including intervening
physical waits. Then

\[
V_i(\text{Defer then Send}\mid h,\omega)
 \le q(h,\omega)S_i(h)+(1-q(h,\omega))(R_i-D_i)
 \le S_i(h).                                      \tag{4}
\]

The second inequality uses S_i(h)>=0 and D_i>R_i. It does not assume q is
measurable in the owner's information: (4) is pointwise before averaging.
Defer followed by Never instead yields at most R_i-D_i<0. The prescribed
Open is consequently a best response against every belief at this root.
Only Send is the limiting continuation at the late callback; its own
rationality is proved next, rather than assumed.

**The late callback.** One prescribed Send continuation, with protected
transport and copied source choices after success and the constructed
failure equilibrium otherwise, has utility at least

\[
-(1-q)M_i
=-(1-q)(N_iD_i+K_i).
\]

Success has nonnegative utility; every failure continuation has utility at
least -M_i. Never, against every later continuation policy, has utility at
most R_i-D_i because it causes i's first failure. Thus the prescribed Send
exceeds any Never continuation by at least

\[
D_i\bigl[1-N_i(1-q)\bigr]-R_i-(1-q)K_i.          \tag{5}
\]

This expression increases with q. The conditional bound q>=q_min and (1)
make it strictly positive, pointwise in every compatible hidden callback
history. Averaging preserves that strict comparison. This proves the local
Send comparison against Never; it does not claim that arbitrary bad policies
after Send dominate every Never policy.

All other no-failure owned menus contain only silence. We have proved every
local one-shot comparison, and the assessment is globally consistent by the
single spliced sequence. Perfect recall and the finite one-shot-deviation
principle give sequential optimality against every adaptive whole policy,
including policies that use physical observations later or deliberately
enter a failure continuation. This completes the SE proof. Initialized play
never defers or fails, so source erasure gives the exact joint terminal law
and no deduction. QED.

## Public parameter feasibility and limits

For a player with N_i>=1, put a_i=1-N_i(1-q_min). If a_i>0, finite constants
chosen before the service can satisfy (1), for example any

\[
D_i>
\max\left\{R_i,\frac{R_i+(1-q_{\min})K_i}{a_i}\right\}.
\]

Thus q_min>1-1/N_i is sufficient under these deliberately coarse total-loss
bounds. For N_i=1, every q_min>0 suffices, reproducing the one-late immutable
disclosure bound. If q_min=1, D_i>R_i suffices. The class includes arbitrarily
reliable accepted late sends; their failure risk is not needed to deter a
separate signaling gain because there is no fresh value choice or intermediate
logical decision on their successful route.

The bound uses total possible future losses to force Send at an already
deferred callback. It is not a necessity theorem. If the first failure
terminates all further penalties, the same proof can use the smaller actual
M_i=D_i+K_i and the multi-phase result needs only q_min>0. Conversely, a fixed
logical source horizon supplies a finite N_i but does not automatically give
one physical late callback, a conditional service bound, or an affordable
deposit. Increasing K_i with D_i requires resolving the resulting joint
inequalities; K_i cannot be held fixed in the algebra and then silently
replaced by a deposit-dependent quantity.

The sticky public failure marker is used for the continuation construction.
Without separation of no-failure and failure information sets, arbitrary
failure equilibria cannot be glued by this proof. A different joint consistency
argument might still work. The assumption is a sufficient structural boundary,
not proof that its every weakening destroys preservation.

## A second interface: publicly reconstructible success odds

There is a different sufficient interface that needs no positive q_min.
It requires more operational information, rather than better delivery. This
assumption is not implied by a scheduler reading a public mempool, by miners
being noncolluding, or by the public source horizon.

**Source and physical model for this result.** Keep the same finite serial
mandatory-opening source, protected opaque binding menus, fixed immutable
late Send/Never callback, full observation recall, terminal gross bounds and
total-loss caps. Keep public failure marking and all listed cost/fault
omissions. Replace the lower success bound by the following exact condition.

At a callback for instruction j, there is an actual recorded public service
state p and a common-knowledge function q_j(p,v) in [0,1]. Conditional on
**every** compatible complete hidden callback history with that p and
immutable value v, Send has success probability exactly q_j(p,v). The owner
knows p and v at the callback. After success, every later player with a
non-singleton strategic menu can recover the callback's p, its late route and
v before choosing. Those records remain in recall. Thus any classification
computed from q_j(p,v) can be recognized at every later genuine decision.

It is insufficient that only the environment had p, that only an unconditional
average q is public, or that the inclusion receipt reveals a different later
state. The native readiness token's instruction identifier, for example,
does not by itself record callback time or full callback service state. One
cannot infer this extra information from its authenticity.

**Paper theorem for this interface.** Suppose D_i>R_i and

\[
D_i^2-(N_i+1)R_iD_i-R_iK_i\ge0.                \tag{6}
\]

Choose those constants before selecting the service. Every source SE has a
preserving target SE for every service in this publicly reconstructible class,
even if some late sends succeed with probability zero or one. The protected
source-copy policy is unchanged. Late continuation policies can depend on the
known q_j and on the selected service's failure game. A single robust complete
behavioral policy under unspecified inclusion laws is not claimed.

**Proof: two overlapping incentive regions.** Write M_i=N_iD_i+K_i and put

\[
H_i=1-\frac{D_i-R_i}{M_i},
\qquad
L_i=1-\frac{R_i}{D_i}.
\]

Because M_i>=D_i>R_i>=0, these are valid thresholds, and (6) is exactly
H_i<=L_i. Call a callback **high** when q_j(p,v)>=H_i and **low** otherwise.
At a high callback, the prescribed Send continuation has utility at least
-(1-q)M_i. Every Never continuation has utility at most R_i-D_i. The
difference is nonnegative by q>=H_i. Send can therefore be prescribed there,
including at a boundary tie.

At a low callback, q<H_i<=L_i. Every continuation after Send has utility
at most R_i-(1-q)D_i<=0: its gross reward is at most R_i, and failure
collects at least D_i. Never has utility at most R_i-D_i<0. Mixtures satisfy
the same bound. Thus every continuation policy at a low callback has utility
at most zero, irrespective of later equilibrium choices. The callback need
not be forced to Send.

**Proof: cutset and consistency.** Before the first low callback or high
failure, use the source-copy strategy, protected Open, and Send at high
callbacks. Treat a low callback itself, or a first failed Send/Never at a high
callback, as a continuation entry. Everything thereafter belongs to the
arbitrary continuation, even when a low Send succeeds. Entry status never
returns to the source-copy region.

A low callback is recognized by its owner. After a low success, its recorded
p, route and public value make that entry recognizable to every later genuine
decision maker. After failure the public marker does so. Hence no
non-singleton information set contains both an entry continuation and a
source-copy history. Other players can remain unaware during forced silent
waits. For the strategic proof, contract singleton response nodes, retain
their observations in all later views, and keep the resulting finite chance
processing. Equal later full recall preserves original strategic information
and actions, so this contraction preserves perfect recall and every real
strategy choice. The entry and source-copy information sets in the contracted
game are disjoint.

Use the source sequence sigma_n. Before entry use type-independent
epsilon_n deferrals and Never trembles at high callbacks. Stop replay at
each entry. The successful source-copy prefixes have the same subprobability
factorization (2). Their coupled replay discards low callbacks as well as
high failures. The discarded classification is identical on paired inputs:
at the current callback the owner knows its fixed value; at every later
source decision earlier successful values and the recorded public p agree.
The joint public-service and remembered-view induction therefore gives the
same fiber equality and projected source beliefs (3).

The mixed prefix induces an actual positive entry prior nu_n when the entry
cutset is nonempty; if it is empty, omit continuation completion. The one-root
continuation construction applies unchanged to the contracted game: include
all full entry histories and prior recall, choose a continuation SE at nu_n,
take sufficiently close fully mixed strategy-and-belief witnesses, splice
them, and take one common compact subsequence. Rationality at all continuation
sites follows by continuity. Reinsert the contracted singleton nodes with
their sole silence action. The same global mixed sequence, and another common
belief subsequence if needed, supplies their consistency; their rationality
is automatic. No convenient callback posterior has been inserted.

Here an arbitrary continuation means rational policies in the original
target menus from each entry. The analysis does not add a new physical
failure flag or any new action after a low callback succeeds; its permanently
recoverable public record supplies the separation used by the proof.

**Proof: the remaining local comparisons.** Genuine source binding comparisons
still erase to sigma under the source projected belief. High callbacks satisfy
their Send comparison by the threshold calculation. At a protected opening,
Open yields the nonnegative source continuation S_i(h), conditional on the
full hidden history. A Defer route ending at a high callback has successful
source continuation S_i(h) and failed utility at most R_i-D_i, so its value
is at most S_i(h). A Defer route ending at a low callback has value at most
zero against its arbitrary completed continuation, also at most S_i(h).
Average over the intervening physical wait histories. Open is locally
optimal. Every continuation site was completed rationally, and singleton
sites have no comparison. Global consistency and finite perfect recall give
full sequential rationality. Initialized play stays protected and preserves
the exact source terminal and utility law. QED.

One simple uniform choice satisfying (6) is

\[
D_i>(N_i+1)R_i+K_i.
\]

This is not a necessity or an affordability bound. It illustrates that
source-copy preservation can tolerate arbitrarily bad late inclusion when
the off-path callback is classified using its genuine public operational
law: reliable callbacks can use fixed-value Send, and unreliable ones can
be completed without becoming a profitable protected-root alternative.

An independent mathematical review accepted this construction and the
threshold algebra. Its additional public-record and known-service conditions
are not established for the native runtime. An unknown persistent service
with only a common reliability property does not supply q_j(p,v). Nor does
this theorem imply that an arbitrary informational weakening breaks
preservation. It is a second sufficient interface, with a different explicit
information requirement.

## Relationship to the concrete runtime

The native serial graph, opaque registration and canonical binding envelope,
full remembered observations, first-turn protected admission, and authentic
fixed-value openings are relevant checked ingredients. The
[native observation criterion](native-observation-criterion.md) identifies
them and the remaining replay adapter. Nothing here makes pending messages
invisible, asks a builder to keep plaintext secret, or releases an opening
without a packet.

The theorem still changes the strategic interface. Native raw play permits
multiple physical callbacks, different pending candidates, premature opening
material and other packets. The retained risk menu can permit an unaccepted
binding to select a value at a late callback. None is covered by the fixed
immutable Send/Never menu above. The
[binding-choice counterexample](admission-information-boundary.md) shows why
that distinction matters in an abstract failure-utility model; its private
attempt-dependent failure utility is not a proved native embedding.

This theorem also does not reuse the checked terminal-audit extension without
checking its hypotheses. That extension needs zero audit on every retained
history. A dead late canonical packet can already be charged by the actual
settled audit, so an interface retaining it as an uncharged clean action
would violate audit soundness. Here all such extra deductions are included in
the declared K_i on the marked failure tails, and consistency is constructed
directly. A full raw extension needs actual matching, collection and payoff
bounds for its complete menus. No native raw adapter is claimed.

The next concrete questions are therefore narrow: can additional callbacks
be simulated without introducing a new value-selection or information
choice; which source failure continuations can satisfy the public separation
and loss bounds; and can the protected binding menus themselves be enlarged
without moving the binding choice to a failure-sensitive late stage? These
are separate from the successful-prefix information argument now proved for
this finite interface.

## Result card

- **Result:** Paper exact forward SE preservation for finite public serial
  programs with protected opaque value bindings, mandatory immutable openings,
  one late Send/Never callback, retained correlated secrets and publicly marked
  finite failure continuations. Full global consistency is constructed.
- **Service and observations:** Arbitrary bounded public-history processing,
  readable pending plaintext, remembered correlated passive samples, conditional
  success q>=q_min and unchanged logical chance. Canonical-envelope coupling
  proves the whole successful-prefix channel identity.
- **Separate public-odds interface:** No success bound is needed when exact
  scalar callback success odds are common knowledge and their public callback
  state, route and value are retained by every later strategic actor. A larger
  uniform forfeit separates reliable callbacks from unprofitable ones; the
  latter can have arbitrary rational continuations even after success.
- **Quantifiers and costs:** Public horizon, utility bounds, loss caps and
  q_min first; fixed failure forfeits next; every permitted service and selected
  source SE thereafter. No initialized deduction. Fundability, fees, financing,
  producer incentives, external channels and coalitions are excluded.
- **Scope:** One late immutable opening is a genuine multi-phase positive;
  late bindings, repeated callbacks, lawful source withholding and full raw
  actions remain outside it. The proof is not a checked native theorem.
- **Review:** Independent mathematical and native interface reviews accepted
  the complete consistency, rationality, coupling and uniform-parameter proof.
  The runtime assembly and machine checking are absent.
